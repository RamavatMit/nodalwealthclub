# Reporting a vulnerability

Thank you for helping keep Nodal Wealth Club members safe.

**Email:** <PLACEHOLDER: security@ address> (also published at `/.well-known/security.txt`)

Please include what you found, steps to reproduce, and the impact you think it has. Do not include other people's personal data.

## What we promise
- Acknowledge your report within **2 working days**.
- Give you a status update within **7 days**.
- Not take legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy.
- Credit you (if you wish) once the issue is fixed.

## Please don't
- Access, change or delete data that isn't yours; stop and report as soon as you see any.
- Run denial-of-service, spam, social-engineering or physical tests.
- Test against other members' accounts, wallets or payments.
- Publicly disclose before we've had a reasonable time (90 days) to fix it.

## In scope
The website and its APIs on our production domain.

## Out of scope
Third-party services (Razorpay, Cloudflare, email and video providers) — report to them directly. Missing headers on non-HTML responses, self-XSS, and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
